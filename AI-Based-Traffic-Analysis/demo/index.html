How to read this demo

The M1, M2 and M3 pages replay traffic through simple rules in your browser (a simulation). The Results page shows numbers measured in the project's Mininet/Ryu/ONOS experiments. Neither is a live controller.

Main finding: the min-max LP gives the gain (about 15% lower mean MLU on Abilene replay). Which forecaster feeds it makes almost no difference, and a stale controller view up to 20 s costs nothing measurable.
